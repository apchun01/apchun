ABOUT

Hi! I'm Alex. These days, I'm an American Studies PhD candidate at Brown University, where I am also pursuing certificates in both Gender and Sexuality Studies and Digital Humanities.

RESEARCH

I research and teach on critical approaches to sex in the digital age, picking up a queer of color critique to trace the entanglements of race, sex, and digital technologies in the 21st century.

My ongoing dissertation project draws on contemporary Asian/American media and film to study how queer masculinities are managed and negotiated through digitally-mediated sexual encounters. Mining the ethical opacities, dissatisfactions, and anti-social impulses latent in the digital age, my work tarries with those who reach for an alternative queer sexual politic.

I previously worked as an assistant editor for Camera Obscura: Feminism, Culture, and Media Studies. In 2026, I won the Best Graduate Student Essay Award from the Association for Asian American Studies.

Outside of my academic endeavors, other things I enjoy include: making music, Seoul, the color blue, and papaya salad.

TEACHING

AMST0192: Sex in the Digital Age. Brown University. Instructor of Record.

This undergraduate seminar of my own design utilizes critical feminist, queer, and digital media studies approaches to intimacy, belonging, and desirability in our increasingly digitized world. Tracing both the lineages and trajectories of sex in the digital age, topics of conversation range from feminist debates about the ethics of pornography in the 70s and 80s to the rise of OnlyFans in the neo-liberal gig economy. We examine racially-biased algorithms and the politics of desire in dating apps, as well as cultural anxieties surrounding artificial intelligence and technological futures, comparing such anxieties with present day and future realities.
